CONFOUND (the one residual, stated honestly)
  spark-arena does NOT pin a vLLM version: spark-vllm-docker builds the latest vLLM at image-build time, so
  every leaderboard entry runs whatever vLLM was current at its submission date. This entry: 2026-05-05.
  Ours: vLLM dated 2026-06-08 (~1 month newer). The +10.4% is most plausibly vLLM decode improvements over
  that month, NOT the OS/kernel/driver. Claim is "reproduced the published recipe on the zero-patch stack,"
  NOT "Rocky is faster." A fully-controlled A/B would require pinning the entry-date vLLM; the leaderboard
  itself does not do this, so cross-date entries are inherently runtime-version-drifted.

NOTE: flash_attn attention backend (recipe default) built and ran on sm_121 (GB10) under the zero-patch stack.
