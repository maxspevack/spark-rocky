DRIVER-ONLY A/B — open driver 580.173.02 (LTSB) vs 610.43.03, PAGE SIZE HELD AT 4k
====================================================================================
Question: does falling back from the 610 branch to the 580 LTSB branch cost performance?
Motivation: 580 is the only branch without the 64k DMA-submap defect (open-driver #1269),
so "ship 580 to get 64k pages" was a live proposal (#80). This prices it.

VERDICT: NO. 580.173.02 is ~10% slower than 610.43.03 across the whole matrix.
         The 580 route to 64k is REJECTED on evidence.

  full-matrix median 580/610 : 0.896   (-10.4%)
  decode  (tg*,  n=28)      : 0.911   (-8.9%)
  prefill (pp/ctx, n=76)    : 0.894   (-10.6%)

  cells >5% SLOWER on 580 : 94 / 104
  cells >5% FASTER on 580 : 0 / 104
  within +/-5%            : 10 / 104

  Not a single cell is faster on 580. The regression is uniform, not workload-specific.

WHY THIS KILLS THE 580 ROUTE TO 64k
  64k's best-case gain (2026-06-16 matrix, and that was measured on a stack carrying the
  #1269 defect): median +2.3%, best cells 1.30-1.38x.
  580's cost to obtain 64k: median -10.4%.
  Net: roughly -8 percentage points of median throughput to buy a +2.3% page-size win.
  A net loss by a wide margin, even taking the June number at face value.
