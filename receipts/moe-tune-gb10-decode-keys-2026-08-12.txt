     on unified memory (~25-235 MB/config, history-dependent); reserved-vs-avail discriminator
     proved the retention below the allocator. Unreported upstream (symptom search 2026-08-11).
  2. A shared-pool fix was A/B-convicted of altering capture-time dispatch (kernel load inside
     capture -> CUDA "operation not permitted"; pure upstream ran the same configs clean).
  3. Final methodology: graph-free eager timing (argmin-preserving; cross-validated against
     graph timing on batch 2 — same block geometry) + one fresh process per batch-size bar +
     top-16 re-race per bar (re-time finalists back-to-back in one thermal window).
  4. The re-race PAID on its first live bar: scan winner != re-race winner at M=8 (8h scan
     window drift). The re-race winner ships. [rerace] log line banked.
  5. NEW Triton compiler defect found at M=8: 48 configs — exactly the BLOCK_SIZE_M=256
     families (256/64/64, 256/128/128, 256/256/256; 16 stage/warp variants each) — crash the
     MLIR pass pipeline (TritonGPURemoveLayoutConversions) at the M=8 specialization while
     compiling FINE at M=1..4. First hit killed a 4h bar at idx 1632; rerun with log-and-skip
     banked all 48 as receipts (skips.log).
SCOPE DECISION (Max, 2026-08-12): stopped after M=8. Consumption analysis: decode at

batch sizes no present workload touches, at ~24-30h more box time. Two-way door.
