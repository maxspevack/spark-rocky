MEASUREMENT-INTEGRITY RECORD (why 14 cells are indicative)
  Both full-matrix strategies were driven to their limits: the chunked run's deep segments
  throttled (seg-04..seg-10; all segments completed rc=0), and the single-cell rerun — one cell
  per serve window, cool-to-<=50C starts — still throttled every attempted cell beyond the
  receipt-grade set (1-145 slowdown samples; traces archived with the artifacts). A cool-to-45C
  retry of the three marginal cells is recorded in the addendum. During the rerun campaign,
  after ~10 cumulative hours at the thermal edge, the box hard-hung silently (no oops or panic
  captured; pstore empty; the journal stops mid-normal-operation at 05:01 UTC; required a
  physical power cycle — evidence and pattern-tracking in #62). The five heaviest single-cell
  re-attempts were closed by decision rather than re-risking the box unattended; their
  chunked-segment values stand as the indicative record.
